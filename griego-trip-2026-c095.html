Where you'll stay
★★★★½

Fairmont El San Juan Hotel

Isla Verde Beach · San Juan, Puerto Rico · Beachfront

Signature Ocean View · 2 Queen Beds

The Fairmont El San Juan sits directly on Isla Verde's Atlantic-facing shore — one of the island's longest stretches of open beach — roughly 10 minutes from the airport and about 20 minutes by cab from Old San Juan's cobblestone plazas. This isn't a boutique escape; it's a full-service resort built around the water, with the personality to match.

Your Signature Ocean View room puts the Atlantic straight ahead: two queen beds, generous proportions, and unobstructed sightlines from the moment you open the curtains. The view doesn't require a corner upgrade or an app-based lottery — it's the category. Full resort access is included, covering pools, beach service, and multiple dining venues on-property.

When you want to leave the grounds, Isla Verde's strip of restaurants, beach bars, and shops is walkable. Old San Juan — the 16th-century walled city with its fortresses, rum distilleries, and narrow streets — is a short ride west and worth at least one full day with the family.
