The Decision

Same Week, Same Cruise Line — Two Very Different Sailings

Both leave South Florida on January 3 and bring you back seven nights later. What happens in between is where they part ways.

Celebrity Beyond sails east out of Fort Lauderdale toward the Dominican Republic, St. Thomas, and St. Kitts — three ports, three full sea days, and a lot of time to actually use the ship. Celebrity Xcel sails out of Miami toward Nassau, Grand Cayman, Cozumel, and Costa Maya — four ports in seven nights, a busier rhythm, and Celebrity's newest ship under your feet.

There's no wrong answer here. One is built around the ship; the other is built around the ports. Below you'll find what each does best, the numbers side by side, and a selector that tallies pricing as you compare.

Option One

Celebrity Beyond

Round-trip Fort Lauderdale (Port Everglades) · Eastern Caribbean

Ports of Call

Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic · Charlotte Amalie, St. Thomas · Basseterre, St. Kitts

Why Beyond

  • Three full sea days. Time to settle into the Sunset Bar, the resort deck, and Eden without watching the clock for an all-aboard.
  • A proven favorite. Beyond has been sailing since 2022 and consistently ranks among Celebrity's best-reviewed ships — the crew and service are dialed in.
  • Infinite Veranda balconies. Beyond's signature cabin design opens the whole room to the water at the touch of a button.
  • A quieter Eastern route. Puerto Plata is a newer, less-trafficked call, and St. Kitts trades crowds for green mountains and calm beaches.

Option Two

Celebrity Xcel

Round-trip Miami · Bahamas & Western Caribbean

Ports of Call

Nassau, Bahamas · Georgetown, Grand Cayman · Cozumel, Mexico · Costa Maya, Mexico

Why Xcel

  • Celebrity's newest ship. Xcel debuts in late 2026 — this sailing puts you aboard during her very first winter season, when everything is brand new.
  • Four ports in seven nights. If you'd rather be off the ship exploring than lounging on it, this is the busier itinerary.
  • Water-first destinations. Grand Cayman's Seven Mile Beach and Stingray City, Cozumel's reefs, and Costa Maya's beach clubs make this the swim-and-snorkel route.
  • The lower fare. A balcony runs $36 less per person than Beyond — $72 back in your pocket as a couple.
