Good to Know

Before you decide

Parking is the only resort extra

There is no resort fee and no cleaning fee here. The one charge outside your package is parking for your SUV, $30 per night, paid directly to the hotel at check-in or check-out. The resort may change the parking fee without notice.

Checked bags

One checked bag per traveler is included with these fares. Additional bags are charged by the airline.

Overnight flight home

You leave Maui at 6:53 p.m. Friday and land in Houston at 8:11 a.m. Saturday after a one-hour connection in Dallas. An overnight return is the norm from Hawaii to the mainland, and most carriers schedule their flights this way, so you keep a full last day on the island.

Cancellation terms

The hotel permits cancellation up to 14 days before arrival. Package cancellation penalties may still apply unless travel protection is purchased. Travel protection does not cover penalties on non-refundable room rates.
