Your Hotel

Casa Marina Key West

Curio Collection by Hilton · 1500 Reynolds Street, near Duval

The Property

Built in 1921 by Henry Flagler, Casa Marina is the oldest resort in Key West — a Spanish Colonial building that carries real architectural weight on a stretch of private beach at the quieter, residential end of the island. The Duval Street action is a 10-minute walk north; the trade-off is beach access you won't find on most Key West properties, and a pace that's noticeably calmer than the center of town.

Location & Walkability

Reynolds Street puts you within comfortable walking distance of the Southernmost Point marker, Fort Zachary Taylor State Park, and the southern end of Duval. The Key West International Airport (EYW) is roughly 2 miles away — close enough for a short taxi or rideshare without the parking headache. The neighborhood is residential and low-key; if you want noise until 2 a.m., you'll walk to it rather than sleep through it.

On the Property

The resort's private sandy beach is the headline feature — loungers, umbrellas, and towels provided, with a beach bar directly on-site. Two outdoor pools offer an alternative when the Atlantic is choppy. Water activities departing from or near the property include snorkeling, sailing, and water skiing; parasailing and windsurfing are available a short distance away. A full health club and yoga classes round out the active options on-site.

Your Room
Room · 2 Queen Beds · Balcony · Ocean View

Two queen beds make this configuration sensible for a group of four — adults and a teenager can spread out without anyone landing on a pull-out sofa. The balcony faces the ocean; at this property that means actual salt air and a view of the Atlantic rather than a parking structure, which is not a given in Key West. The ocean view rooms are positioned on the south side of the building, which captures afternoon light and the best sightlines down to the beach.

Budget accordingly if you plan to eat at the hotel most mornings; Key West also has no shortage of breakfast spots within walking distance if you prefer to explore.
