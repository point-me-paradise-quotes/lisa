Planned around the two of you

What I kept in mind for you both

Gretchen's shellfish allergy

Your allergy will be recorded on your booking so the dining team has it before you step aboard, whichever sailing you choose. On the first evening, introduce yourself to the restaurant manager as well, and mention it to your server at each meal. On ships this size the kitchen gets to know you quickly and will steer you to dishes prepared without shellfish.

A steadier trip for Harmon

Late May and early June is usually one of the calmer stretches of the year in the Mediterranean. On the Sicilian Interlude there are no full days at sea: every leg is a short overnight hop between islands, so most of the sailing happens while you sleep and your days are spent ashore. Whichever sailing you choose, I will request a stateroom as low and as close to the middle of the ship as your category allows, since that is where motion is felt least. Bring along whatever remedy has worked for you before, and the onboard medical center can help if you need it.

A quiet, grown-up atmosphere

None of these ships is adults-only, but children are rare on all three sailings, typically around 1% of guests. On the Ponant sailings, the few young travelers on board are usually teenagers between 14 and 18. Explora does run a youth club on some voyages, though mid-May, before most schools let out, is one of the quietest times of year for families. On all three ships the pace on board is relaxed and conversation-friendly.

My recommendationPalermo to Valletta

Sicilian Interlude

Aboard Le Boréal
May 28 to June 8, 2027 5-night cruise, 6 ports 3 nights Palermo, 2 nights Valletta

Why this is the right fit for you

This is the trip you described to me. You land in Palermo and spend three nights there before you ever board, which puts you in Sicily's capital over Memorial Day weekend with time for the markets, the Arab-Norman churches and the evening passeggiata. When you sail, four of the next five stops are Sicilian: Trapani for the hilltop town of Erice, the Aeolian island of Lipari, a long day in Taormina that runs until 10:30 at night, and Siracusa with the island of Ortygia and the Greek theater.

Le Boréal is a small French ship built for exactly this kind of coastline, and the fare carries an open bar, gratuities, Wi-Fi and one included shore excursion per guest in every port. You finish with two nights in Valletta, Malta, so the trip ends at an easy pace instead of a rush to the airport.

Barcelona is not on this route, but it does not have to be left out. I can extend this trip with a flight to Barcelona, either before you arrive in Palermo or after Malta, so you still get your day at the Sagrada Família. Let me know on the form below and I will price the flight and hotel nights for you.

Barcelona to Rome

Barcelona to Rome

Aboard Explora I
May 14 to May 26, 2027 7-night cruise 2 nights Barcelona, 2 nights Rome

Why it is on your list

You told me you wanted to see the Sagrada Família, and this is the only option that takes you to Barcelona. You arrive two nights ahead of the ship, which gives you a full day for Gaudí's basilica, the Gothic Quarter and La Rambla before you board. Explora I is the most polished ship of the three, designed to feel like a private yacht, and your Ocean Terrace Suite is one of the largest entry-level suites at sea, with unlimited drinks, gratuities and Wi-Fi included.

The trade is Sicily. This route gives you a single day there, in Taormina, and spreads the rest of the week across Ibiza, Tunis, Malta and the Amalfi Coast before two nights in Rome. It also runs about two weeks earlier than you asked for, bringing you home on May 26, before Memorial Day weekend.

Valletta to Nice · With Smithsonian

Mediterranean Island Voyage

Aboard Le Dumont-d'Urville
May 15 to May 28, 2027 8-night cruise, 9 ports 2 nights Malta, 2 nights Nice

Why it is on your list

If you would like a longer voyage with more depth, this is the one. It is a Smithsonian sailing built around the islands of the Mediterranean, and Sicily sits at its center with three back-to-back port days in Siracusa, Taormina and Palermo. From there you continue to Sardinia, Corsica and Elba before finishing on the Riviera in Nice. Le Dumont-d'Urville is an expedition-style small ship with an underwater lounge, and your fare includes an open bar, gratuities, Wi-Fi and one shore excursion per guest in every port.

It asks more of you in two ways. The dates run May 15 to May 28, so you are home just before Memorial Day weekend rather than traveling through it, and it is the highest-priced of the three, roughly double the other two.
