Trip Overview

Park Once, Walk to the Show

Built around a night at Climate Pledge Arena

You asked for a hotel close to the arena, in a neighborhood that feels safe after a late show, at a nicer level without paying downtown rates. The Maxwell sits in Lower Queen Anne, about a six-minute walk from Climate Pledge Arena, where you will see the Pixies and Queens of the Stone Age. Your car stays parked in the hotel's own lot on show night, and you walk home.

Refundable nonstop flights get you into Seattle before 10:00 a.m. on day one, and a rental car is waiting at the airport so the rest of the trip is yours: Pike Place Market, the waterfront, or a drive out of the city if the weather cooperates.
