Portofino Island Resort

📍 10 Portofino Drive, Pensacola Beach · 38 min from PNS · Restaurants within walking distance

Five towers perched at the convergence of the Gulf of Mexico and Santa Rosa Sound, Portofino Island Resort is the most amenity-rich choice in this quote. This is where a 14-year-old has plenty to do without anyone having to drive anywhere — the Adventure Beach center on the Gulf side puts kayaks, paddleboards, and surfboard rentals directly at your feet, while Adventure Cove on the Sound side runs water park-style inflatable slides. For adults, the full spa, tennis courts, and poolside dining handle the slower hours. Named among Southern Living's 10 Best Family-Friendly Beach Hotels in Florida, the property earns that designation through sheer density of on-site experience. Dining at Al Fresco restaurant, dolphin cruise departures, and bike trails round out the calendar — and with your rental car, the rest of the Emerald Coast is within easy reach.

Sans Souci by Southern Vacation Rentals · #108

📍 999 Fort Pickens Rd, Pensacola Beach · 30 min from PNS · 1-minute walk to beach

The closest of the three options to Pensacola Beach's main activity corridor, Sans Souci puts you on Fort Pickens Road with a boardwalk that drops you onto the Gulf in under a minute. The complex sits adjacent to a golf course and right next to Santa Rosa Sound, so the views work in two directions. What you gain here over Portofino is simplicity and self-sufficiency — the condo comes with a fully equipped kitchen, in-unit washer/dryer, free parking, and Wi-Fi, which means grocery runs replace resort restaurant bills. Peg Leg Pete's, one of the more reliable seafood stops on the beach, is walkable. The Pensacola Beach Boardwalk and The Shell (an outdoor live music venue) are 1.7 miles down the strip — a short drive or a committed walk. Unit #108 is ground floor, with an ocean-view balcony.

Ocean Breeze West by Southern Vacation Rentals

📍 14623 Perdido Key Drive, Pensacola · 49 min from PNS · Perdido Key beachfront

Ocean Breeze West is the quietest and most isolated choice in this quote — and intentionally so. The property sits on Perdido Key, a narrower barrier island 49 minutes from PNS, where a 47-unit complex faces the Gulf with roughly 600 feet of vacant beach to the east. There is no street to cross — the Gulf-front pool and sun deck lead directly to the sand. Perdido Key State Park is an 8-minute walk; Flora-Bama Lounge, one of the Gulf Coast's more storied honky-tonks straddling the Florida-Alabama state line, is 7 minutes by car. Lost Key Golf Club is a 3-minute drive. The trade-off is distance from Pensacola Beach's dining and activity strip — you'll want a car or a willingness to stay local. The payoff is near-solitude on the water, and at $3,748.58, the lowest rate in this quote.
