Booked for you

Two outings, one in the city and one in the rainforest

Booked · Sun, Dec 27, 11:00 a.m.

Old San Juan Food Tour with 7 Tastings

A small-group walk of about three hours through the old city, eating as you go: mallorca with guava, pasteles de cerdo, mofongo with chicken, an empanadilla, local chocolate and a cortado made with Puerto Rican mountain-grown coffee. The route passes San Cristóbal, Calle San Sebastián and the Cathedral, so you'll have the lay of the land on day one.

Meet at Plaza Colón, by the fountain at the south end across from Teatro Tapia. Look for the guide with the orange umbrella. A short walk from your hotel.

Booked · Mon, Dec 28, 8:00 a.m.

El Yunque Waterfall Hike

A small group of no more than 13, picked up at El Colonial. On the east side of the rainforest you'll hike about 30 minutes to a natural pool for a swim, then another 15 minutes up to a second pool with waterfalls. Rope swings and cliff jumps are there for anyone who wants them, and entirely optional.

Paths can be muddy and a little rocky, so wear shoes with good grip and bring a swimsuit, towel and a change of clothes. Mornings are the best time in the forest, before the afternoon showers.

After dark

Live music for a grown-up crowd

About 10 minutes by taxi

La Placita de Santurce

A working market square by day with local food stands. Thursday through Sunday evenings it turns into a street party with salsa and live bands, and the crowd spans every age. Taberna Los Vázquez on the square is a local favorite for live salsa.

Walk from the hotel

La Factoría

Salsa and bachata in a string of connected rooms, each with its own feel. It fills up later in the evening.

Walk from the hotel

El Batey

An old-school neighborhood bar that has been part of the old city for decades. No-frills, with a jukebox and a mix of locals and visitors.

Holiday schedules shift around New Year's. We'll confirm music nights and hours for every venue before you leave.
