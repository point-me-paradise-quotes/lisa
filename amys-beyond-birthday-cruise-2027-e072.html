The Trip

Everyone together, nobody cooking

This is the birthday plan: seven adults and one very portable baby fly from Atlanta to Fort Lauderdale on the morning of January 17 and board Celebrity Beyond that afternoon. Over the next seven nights the ship handles the logistics — three islands, three sea days, and dinner reservations that don't require a group text. You dock back in Fort Lauderdale on the morning of the 24th and fly home that afternoon.

Beyond is the ship to do this on. Your three Edge Staterooms each have a veranda wall that opens at the touch of a button, there are more than a dozen included and specialty restaurants aboard, and sea days come with a rooftop garden, an adults-only Solarium, and a casino for whoever's feeling lucky. The itinerary alternates port days and sea days, so nobody comes home needing a vacation from the vacation.

We don't book hotels. We build trips. Custom guides, trip app, real human when something goes sideways. The work you'd hate doing — already done.

Day by Day

The itinerary

Sun · Jan 17

Atlanta → Fort Lauderdale → set sail

Ship departs 4:00 p.m.

Morning flight lands at 10:20 a.m. Board Celebrity Beyond, drop your bags, and be on deck with a drink in hand as Florida slides behind you.

Mon · Jan 18

Nassau, Bahamas

In port 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.

The harbor sits a block from Bay Street's shops and cafés. Duty-free boutiques, Bahamian craft stalls, pink colonial government buildings, and Fort Charlotte for the history crowd — or a horse-drawn carriage tour to cover it all without breaking a sweat.

Tue · Jan 19

At sea

First full sea day. Claim a lounger at the Rooftop Garden, book the spa, or send the grown-ups to the adults-only Solarium while the pool deck's live music runs from sunup to sundown.

Wed · Jan 20

Cozumel, Mexico

In port 7:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m.

Thirteen hours in port — the longest stop of the week. The Mesoamerican Reef makes this one of the Caribbean's best snorkel and dive stops, San Gervasio covers the Mayan ruins, and San Miguel's waterfront handles lunch and shopping.

Thu · Jan 21

At sea

Recovery day. Late breakfast, trivia, a matinee in The Theatre, and a proper dinner at one of the four included main restaurants — Cyprus, Tuscan, Normandie, or Cosmopolitan.

Fri · Jan 22

George Town, Grand Cayman

In port 8:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.

Stroll Harbour Drive along the waterfront, visit the 1919 Peace Memorial and the Wall of History, and let the kids-at-heart pose with the blue iguana statues. Stingray City and Seven Mile Beach are the classic excursions — ask and we'll set them up.

Sat · Jan 23

At sea

Last full day aboard. The Magic Carpet — a floating bar platform on the ship's side — is the spot for a farewell toast before the final show in The Theatre.

Sun · Jan 24

Fort Lauderdale → Atlanta

Ship arrives 7:00 a.m. · Flight departs 12:15 p.m.

Dock at 7:00 a.m. The airport is a five-minute ride from the pier, so there's no rush — you're home in Atlanta by mid-afternoon.

Good to Know

Before you pack

  • No vaping ashore in Cozumel. Mexico bans vaping devices nationwide — no personal-use exemption, and devices can be confiscated at the pier. Leave them on the ship. Regular cigarettes are treated differently.
  • Traveling with the baby: the infant sails and flies as a lap baby. Bring a birth certificate or passport, and pack diapers and formula from home — port prices are steep and selection is thin.
  • Onboard extras: specialty restaurants, drink packages, and Wi-Fi plans are add-ons. Tell us what your group wants and we'll price the packages before you sail — pre-cruise rates beat onboard rates.
  • Documents: passports are the smoothest option for everyone. If anyone in the group needs a renewal before January, start now.
