Day by day

The Itinerary

  • Day 1 Sat, May 22
    Depart Seattle
    Ship sails at 3:00 p.m.
    Plan to be onboard no later than 1:00 p.m. Settle in, then find a spot on deck as you head north into Puget Sound.
  • Day 2 Sun, May 23
    At Sea
    A full day to learn the ship. Go-karts, the Aqua Park, and the Observation Lounge up front, where the coastline starts turning wild.
  • Day 3 Mon, May 24
    Sitka, Alaska
    10:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m. · Docked
    Russian-era history, Totem Park trails, and sea otter rafts in the sound. One of Alaska's most walkable ports.
  • Day 4 Tue, May 25
    Endicott Arm & Dawes Glacier, then Juneau
    Juneau: 12:30 p.m. – 9:30 p.m. · Docked
    The morning is scenic cruising up Endicott Arm to the face of Dawes Glacier. Then a long afternoon and evening in Juneau: Mendenhall Glacier, the Mount Roberts Tramway, or whale watching out of Auke Bay.
  • Day 5 Wed, May 26
    Icy Strait Point, Alaska
    6:00 a.m. – 3:00 p.m. · Docked
    Home to some of the most reliable whale sightings in Southeast Alaska, plus the gondola up Hoonah Mountain and one of the world's longest ziplines.
  • Day 6 Thu, May 27
    Ketchikan (Ward Cove), Alaska
    6:00 a.m. – 1:15 p.m. · Docked
    An early start in the salmon capital. Creek Street, totem poles, and floatplane flights over Misty Fjords if you want to go big.
  • Day 7 Fri, May 28
    Victoria, British Columbia
    8:00 p.m. – 11:59 p.m. · Docked
    An evening call in Canada's garden city. The Inner Harbour and the illuminated Parliament Buildings are a short stroll from the pier.
  • Day 8 Sat, May 29
    Arrive Seattle
    Ship arrives at 6:00 a.m.
    Breakfast onboard, then disembark. Book flights home for early afternoon or later to keep the morning unhurried.
