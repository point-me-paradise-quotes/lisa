Trip Overview

Snow days, mountain nights

Five nights in Grand County, home in time for Christmas Eve

This is Christmas week in the Colorado mountains: snow on the peaks, cold clear air, and the lights of the season everywhere you look. Your days are yours to fill with skiing, sledding, and getting out into the winter landscape. Your evenings bring you back to a two-bedroom condo in Granby with its own living area and kitchen, so everyone has room to spread out, warm up, and settle in for the night.

You fly out of Hobby on Saturday morning, December 19, pick up a four-wheel-drive SUV at Denver International, and head into the mountains. On Thursday, December 24, you fly nonstop from Denver back to Hobby, landing at 7:55 p.m. on Christmas Eve.
