Your itinerary

Day by day

Times are ship's time and set by the cruise line.

  1. Sun, Jan 17
    Fort Lauderdale, Florida
    Sails 4:00 p.m.

    Board at Port Everglades around midday, drop your bags, and have lunch on deck before sail-away.

  2. Mon, Jan 18
    Nassau, Bahamas
    7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

    The ship docks steps from Bay Street. Walk to the Queen's Staircase and Fort Fincastle, or take a short ride to Cable Beach for the afternoon.

  3. Tue, Jan 19
    At sea

    Your first full day on board. The Rooftop Garden, the Solarium and the spa thermal suite are at their quietest in the morning.

  4. Wed, Jan 20
    Cozumel, Mexico
    7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

    Your longest port day. Snorkel the reef off the island's west side, see the Maya site at San Gervasio, and still be back for a late dinner in town.

  5. Thu, Jan 21
    At sea

    A good night for the birthday dinner. Book a specialty restaurant before you sail so the whole family can sit together.

  6. Fri, Jan 22
    George Town, Grand Cayman
    8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.

    A tender port, so allow time for the boat ride in. Stingray City and Seven Mile Beach are the two classic ways to spend the day.

  7. Sat, Jan 23
    At sea

    A slow last day to pack, settle up and catch the evening show in The Theatre.

  8. Sun, Jan 24
    Fort Lauderdale, Florida
    Arrives 7:00 a.m.

    Disembarkation runs through the morning. Plan any flights home for early afternoon or later.
