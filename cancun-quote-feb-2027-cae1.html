Where You're Staying

Riu Cancun

All inclusive, adults only, on the beach in the Cancun hotel zone

One room for three adults, ocean view. The property sits directly on the sand in the hotel zone, which puts you within a short drive of the restaurants, marina, and nightlife along Kukulkan Boulevard without having to leave the beach to find anything to do. Rated 4.0.

Included at the resort

  • All meals, snacks, and local and imported drinks at every bar and restaurant on property
  • Snacks and drinks available 24 hours
  • Minibar and liquor dispenser in the room, refilled regularly
  • In room safe and WiFi throughout the hotel
  • Beach volleyball, kayaking, snorkel gear, pedal boats, the RiuFit program, and one scuba tryout in the pool
  • Gym and wellness facilities
  • Live music, shows, and the daytime entertainment program for adults
  • Entry to the on site discotheque, Pachata (18 and over)
  • The Riu Party at Riu Caribe, daytime and evening, with transfer included

Exchange privileges

Your wristband also gets you into the bars, lunch, and snacks at Riu Caribe, Riu Dunamar, Riu Ventura, and Riu Latino.

  • Breakfast and dinner are taken at your own hotel
  • Transportation between the sister properties is not included, apart from the Riu Party transfer
  • All guests wear an all inclusive wristband for identification

Airport transfers

Shared round trip transportation between Cancun airport and the hotel zone is included for all three travelers, arrival and departure.

Celebrating

Honeymoon amenities

If this trip is a honeymoon, the resort adds the following at no cost. Tell us and we will note it on the reservation, or enter "HONEYMOON" in the special service request section at check out.

  • A welcome gift in the room and a small bottle of tequila
  • King bed, subject to availability
  • Late check out, subject to availability at time of departure
  • Complimentary upgrade to the next available room category, on request and subject to availability
  • $35 spa credit toward "Senses," a 120 minute hot stone massage and flash facial for two

Proof of marriage is required at check in, with a marriage certificate issued within the last 90 days. The spa credit applies to that specific service only, one credit per service, per couple, per stay, and does not combine with other promotions.
