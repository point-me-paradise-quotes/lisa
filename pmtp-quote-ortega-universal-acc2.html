Inside the Parks

How Your Family Uses Four Days

Your 4-day Park-to-Park tickets let you move freely between all four parks on any day. A note on Volcano Bay: Universal has announced a seasonal closure beginning October 26, 2026 — an early October trip captures all four; November travel substitutes a second day at Epic Universe or IOA.

Day 1
Epic Universe
Universal's newest park (opened 2025). Super Nintendo World, Ministry of Magic, How to Train Your Dragon — Isle of Berk, Dark Universe. Your on-property hotel Early Park Admission gets you in one hour ahead of the public.
Day 2
Wizarding World
Hogsmeade in Islands of Adventure plus Diagon Alley in Universal Studios Florida. Ride the Hogwarts Express between both — included with Park-to-Park tickets.
Day 3
Islands of Adventure
Hagrid's Motorbike Adventure, Velocicoaster, Jurassic World, Doctor Doom. The most ride-dense park — plan to start early and hit the biggest attractions before 10 a.m.
Day 4
Volcano Bay / Flex
Volcano Bay works well as a low-key day (weather dependent, open through Oct. 25). Or use day 4 to revisit Epic Universe — families rarely feel like one day there is enough.

Ticket note for your family: Universal prices anyone age 10 and older as an adult. Both your 16-year-old and your 12-year-old are adult-priced. The 6-ticket total above reflects this correctly — all six guests are at the adult rate of approximately $560 per person for 4-day Park-to-Park All 4 Parks tickets.

A Note on Dining

Universal Doesn't Offer a Dining Plan Anymore

You asked about an all-inclusive food option. Here's the honest answer: Universal Orlando discontinued its dining plan in July 2021 and has not brought it back. The food experience at Universal is excellent — Hogsmeade, Diagon Alley, Mythos, Three Broomsticks, Leaky Cauldron — but it's pay-as-you-go.

Quick-Service Days
~$40–55/person
Burgers, fish and chips, Butterbeers, themed snacks. Budget this for your heaviest park days when you're covering the most ground.
Table-Service Nights
~$35–60/person
Mythos Restaurant, Finnegan's Bar, Confisco Grille. Make reservations 60+ days out. One sit-down dinner per evening adds up but gives the trip some breathing room.
Drinks & Snacks
Budget separately
A Butterbeer runs about $8. Coke Freestyle cups ($17 for unlimited refills all day) are a smart buy for a family this size in Florida heat.
Our Budget Estimate
$65/person/day
Built into both options above. Covers breakfast at the hotel, quick-service lunch, a few snacks, and dinner — no white-tablecloth every night, but a comfortable mix.
