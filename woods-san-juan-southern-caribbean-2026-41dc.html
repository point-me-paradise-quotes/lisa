Ways to spend your San Juan day

Your free day before the cruise is a blank page. Any of these can be added to your package — ask Lisa for current pricing and she'll handle the booking.

Bio Bay night kayak, Fajardo

Paddle Laguna Grande after dark, where microscopic dinoflagellates glow with every stroke. Transport from San Juan included.

El Yunque Rainforest & Luquillo Beach

Natural waterslides in the only tropical rainforest in the U.S. forest system, then an afternoon on Luquillo's calm sand.

Mixology class at Casa BACARDÍ

A welcome cocktail at the Pavilion, the story behind the classics, and the fundamentals of building them yourself.

San Juan food tour

Three hours of single-origin Puerto Rican coffee, chocolate, street food, and mofongo along the old city's cobblestone streets.

Sunset harbor boat ride

An easy evening pontoon cruise around San Juan Bay as the light goes gold behind the city walls.

Round-trip airport transfer

Private van service between SJU and your hotel, both directions — no taxi line on arrival day.
