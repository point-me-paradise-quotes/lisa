Your Cabin

Cabin Option

Connecting rooms, because apparently proximity matters on a 9-day sailing.

Interior Connecting Cabin 4A

Carnival Jubilee · Deck 4

Interior cabins have a quiet logic to them: you're on a ship surrounded by water on all sides — you don't need a window to prove it. What you do need is a connecting door, which is exactly what this category provides. Five adults across connecting interiors means everyone has a bed and nobody has to knock to borrow sunscreen. The Carnival Jubilee is the line's newest Excel-class ship, which translates to more to do, better food options, and the kind of ship design that makes a sea day feel like an event rather than a wait.

The Ports

Where You'll Actually Get Off the Ship

Nassau, Bahamas
Tuesday · 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Nassau is the kind of place that rewards the people who actually walk away from the pier. The colonial architecture in the old town, the fish fry at Arawak Cay, the water so clear it looks like someone forgot to add the realistic detail — all of it is within reach. For a group with graduation energy, the beach clubs at Cable Beach are a strong call. For the parents who'd like a quieter morning, the straw market and Queen's Staircase are a half-hour walk and genuinely interesting.

RelaxAway Beach Club, Half Moon Cay™
Wednesday · 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Carnival owns this island. Which means the beach chairs are already arranged, the water sports are pre-organized, and the bar is open before you've dried off from the tender. Half Moon Cay is consistently ranked among the top private island experiences at sea — not because it's trying hard, but because it doesn't need to. The RelaxAway section ups the ante with a calmer vibe, reserved chairs, and direct cabana access. Bring a book you'll pretend to read.

Celebration Key™
Thursday · 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM

This is Carnival's newest private destination, launched in 2024 in the Bahamas. Where Half Moon Cay leans classic, Celebration Key leans new: a lagoon pool that fits four thousand people without feeling crowded, a beach club, swim-up bars, and a waterslide situation that no one is too old for regardless of what they told themselves this morning. For a graduation group, this is the day that earns the most photos.
