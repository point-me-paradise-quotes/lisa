Your trip at a glance

Downtown base, three anchor experiences, room to breathe

You fly nonstop from Houston Hobby on Wednesday morning and land at LaGuardia before 11 a.m. Four nights at the World Center Hotel put you a few blocks from Wall Street, the 9/11 Memorial and the Seaport, which keeps most of your days walkable and your evenings short on travel.

The plan is built around the three things you asked for: The Lion King on Broadway, a harbor cruise out to the Statue of Liberty, and a walk across the Brooklyn Bridge. Everything else is optional and easy to drop. Each day has one fixed point and plenty of open time, and Saturday stays flexible as a backup in case March weather moves the outdoor plans.

We don't book hotels. We build trips. Custom guides, trip app, real human when something goes sideways. The work you'd hate doing: already done.

Day by day

A suggested plan, not yet booked. Times firm up as each piece is confirmed.

WedMarch 10

Arrival and a first look downtown

  • 10:55 a.m. Land at LaGuardia; your airport transfer is arranged once flights are confirmed.
  • Drop bags at the World Center Hotel (rooms are usually ready mid-afternoon).
  • Walk the Oculus and the outdoor 9/11 Memorial pools, both a few minutes away and free.
  • Check in, rest, and dinner at your own pace nearby.
ThuMarch 11

Midtown and The Lion King

  • Late morning in Central Park, then Rockefeller Center and Fifth Avenue.
  • 5:00 p.m. Early dinner in the Theater District.
  • 7:00 p.m. The Lion King at the Minskoff Theatre, 200 West 45th Street. Plan to be seated by 6:45 p.m.
FriMarch 12

The harbor, then the Brooklyn Bridge

  • 9:30 a.m. Walk about 15 minutes from the hotel to Pier 16 at South Street Seaport.
  • 10:00 a.m. 50-minute narrated harbor cruise out to the Statue of Liberty and back, passing the Brooklyn Bridge, Ellis Island and One World Trade Center. Morning sun is on Lady Liberty's face, and you can step out on deck near Liberty Island for close-up photos. No island landing, no security lines.
  • Short taxi ride to DUMBO for lunch and the classic bridge views from the Brooklyn side.
  • Afternoon Walk the Brooklyn Bridge on your own back toward Manhattan, about 45 minutes at an easy pace. Your trip guide maps the route and the best photo stops.
  • If the forecast turns, the bridge walk moves to Saturday.
SatMarch 13

Your choice day

  • 10:00 a.m. Optional: One World Observatory, timed entry, for the view over everything you have walked so far. Allow about 90 minutes.
  • Afternoon at Chelsea Market and a stroll along the High Line.
  • This is also the backup day for anything weather pushed off Friday.
SunMarch 14

Easy morning, home by evening

  • Clocks move forward one hour overnight (daylight saving time begins), so the morning comes early.
  • Breakfast, then Battery Park or the cobblestones of Stone Street. The weekend before St. Patrick's Day can bring a lively crowd to Stone Street.
  • Collect bags from the hotel.
  • 1:30 p.m. Suggested airport pickup for the 5:35 p.m. flight; timing confirmed closer to travel.
