Your Trip

Unpack once. Let the islands come to you.

Jackie, the first cruise answered the question. This one is the reward. You already know how the days work: slow coffee on the verandah while the ship eases into a new harbor, an afternoon that belongs to nobody but you, a dinner that ends whenever you decide it ends.

Both sailings below leave round trip from Fort Lauderdale in February 2027, both put you in a verandah stateroom with floor to ceiling doors onto your own balcony, and both include the Have It All package: Wi-Fi, specialty dining, a shore excursion credit, and a drink package. Each one also carries $300 in onboard credit to spend however you like.

The real choice is between two different Caribbeans. Option One runs the eastern chain: Sint Maarten, Antigua, Dominica, Martinique, St. Kitts, Tortola. Green mountains, French creole cooking, a new island almost every morning. Option Two runs south to the ABC islands: Bonaire, Curaçao, Aruba. Dutch colonial color, dry coastline, some of the calmest water in the region, and four full days at sea with nothing on the schedule.

What this itinerary gives you

  • Six islands in six consecutive days, each with a full day ashore. This is the itinerary if you want range and don't mind an early alarm.
  • Dominica and Martinique are the standouts: rain forest and waterfalls on one, and on the other a French island where the cooking is actually French, with creole behind it.
  • Rotterdam is a Pinnacle Class ship. The main theater wraps you in a two story panoramic screen, and there is an outdoor movie screen above the Lido pool.
  • Two sea days on the way out and one on the way home, so the pace is not relentless.
