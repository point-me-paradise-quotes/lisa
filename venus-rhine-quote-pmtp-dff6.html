Trip overview

Two nights on land, then seven on the water

You arrive in Budapest on Monday, September 27, and have two nights in the city to shake off the flight before boarding the AmaSonata on Wednesday the 29th. The ship loops out along the Danube and brings you back to Budapest on Wednesday, October 6.

Starting and ending in the same city keeps the logistics simple: one airport, no one-way positioning, and a hotel package already folded into the price.

We don't book hotels. We build trips. Custom guides, trip app, real human when something goes sideways. The work you'd hate doing, already done.

Why this cruise line

Why I chose AmaWaterways for you

Most river lines will take you down the Danube. AmaWaterways is the one I trust for Salzburg and The Sound of Music. Their full-day Salzburg excursion from Linz takes you into the city where the film was shot, and it's part of your fare.

Beyond Salzburg, every port comes with several included excursions run at different paces, so you choose how hard you want each day to be. Wine and beer with lunch and dinner, the specialty restaurant, Wi-Fi and the onboard bikes are all included too. Very little turns into a surprise charge.

Salzburg, Saturday, October 2

The hills, the gardens, the gazebo.

When the ship docks in Linz, you can spend the day in Salzburg, about two hours by coach. It's Mozart's birthplace and the city of The Sound of Music.

Excursions at your pace

Walking tours are run as Gentle, Regular or Active groups. There are late-start options, bike rides and guided hikes as well.

Budapest at both ends

Because the cruise starts and ends in Budapest, you see the city properly instead of rushing through it on the way to the airport.

Drinks and dining covered

Wine, beer and soft drinks are poured freely at lunch and dinner. There's a daily cocktail hour, and the specialty restaurant comes at no extra cost.
