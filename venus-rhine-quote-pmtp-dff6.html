Trip Overview

Europe for the first time, by river

One cabin, one unpack, a new town outside the window each morning.

Both options below sail the same core of the Rhine: Cologne and its twin-spired cathedral, Koblenz where the Moselle joins the Rhine, the vineyard town of Rüdesheim, Speyer, Strasbourg across the French border, and Breisach before you finish in Basel, Switzerland.

The difference is where you begin, the ship and the room. River cruising suits a first trip to Europe because the logistics disappear. Your room travels with you, the ship docks close to town, and your group stays together without anyone playing tour manager.

We don't book hotels. We build trips. Custom guides, trip app, real human when something goes sideways. The work you'd hate doing, already done.

Option A · Viking Sigyn

Amsterdam to Basel, November 2 to 9, 2027

8 days, 9 ports. You start among Amsterdam's canals and the Kinderdijk windmills before the river carries you south through Germany and France to Switzerland.

Veranda Stateroom

  • 205 sq ft, with floor-to-ceiling sliding doors onto a full private veranda
  • Queen-size bed, or twins on request, with robes and slippers
  • Glass-enclosed shower and heated bathroom floor
  • Free Wi-Fi, bottled water replenished daily, twice-daily housekeeping
  • 110 and 220 volt outlets plus USB ports, so your U.S. chargers work as they are

On board

  • One included guided tour in every port, with optional tours available at extra cost
  • Beer, wine and soft drinks with lunch and dinner; coffee, tea and bottled water around the clock
  • Aquavit Terrace at the bow for meals outdoors and drinks with your group
  • The Lounge, with a pianist at cocktail hour and evening trivia and entertainment
  • Sun Deck with a walking track, putting green and herb garden

Option B · MS Oscar Wilde

Cologne to Basel, October 26 to November 2, 2027

8 days, 7 ports. You begin in Cologne and follow the same Rhine route south, with drinks included all day and a full day in Lucerne and the Swiss Alps before you finish in Basel.

Standard Suite, Ruby (Middle) Deck

  • 183 sq ft, with floor-to-ceiling glass doors and a French balcony
  • Twin beds that convert to a double
  • Nespresso machine and tea-making set
  • Walk-in shower, bathrobes, slippers and hair dryer
  • Safe, mini fridge, flat-screen TV and nightly turndown

On board

  • Guided excursions with local guides in every port, several with a choice of tour
  • All-inclusive drinks throughout the day: house wine, draught beer, spirits, cocktails, soft drinks, tea and coffee
  • Main Restaurant with tables for four or six, plus an open-kitchen Bistro you can reserve on board at no extra charge
  • Panorama Lounge and Bar for watching the river with your group
  • Fitness centre, spa treatments and hairdresser on the lower deck
  • Sun Deck with a splash pool and putting green, and a few bicycles to borrow

Good to know: outlets on this ship are U.K. style, so pack a plug adapter. The Sun Deck is reached by stairs only.
