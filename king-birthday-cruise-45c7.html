Sailing A · 7 nights · our recommendation

MSC Seascape

More nights, more ports, lower total

A week changes the shape of a trip. By the third morning you've stopped checking the time, and by the fifth you've found your favorite corner of the ship. Seascape has plenty of them, from quiet spots by the rail to the Robotron ride you can see in the photo, if your birthday calls for a little adrenaline. Three ports give you three different afternoons, and two sea days give you the space between them.

Itinerary · ports in order

  • Sun, Jan 10Galveston, Texas · depart
  • Day at sea
  • Costa Maya, Mexico
  • Roatán, Honduras
  • Cozumel, Mexico
  • Day at sea
  • Sun, Jan 17Galveston, Texas · return

Your advisor will confirm the date for each port. Costa Maya is the quieter Mexican stop, with beach clubs near the pier. Roatán is in Honduras's Bay Islands, known for its reef. Cozumel rounds it out with clear water and the town of San Miguel.

Carnival Miracle

The shorter option

If time away is tight, four nights still makes a good birthday: a sea day to settle in, a day in Cozumel, a sea day to do nothing at all. Just know you're paying a little more in total for three fewer nights and two fewer ports than the MSC week.

Good to know

What sits outside the price

Getting to the ship Not included

The drive from Houston to the Galveston cruise terminal and back, and parking at the port, are not part of either price. Your advisor can arrange port parking for you.

Ashore and onboard Not included

Shore excursions, specialty dining, spa visits and other onboard purchases beyond what's listed for each sailing are not included. The $100 onboard credit on MSC Seascape can help with some of these.

Travel insurance Not included

Not part of either price. Let us know below if you'd like a quote.

Travel documents

Both sailings visit Mexico, and the MSC Seascape sailing also visits Honduras. Your advisor will confirm which documents to bring. For current official guidance, check travel.state.gov before you sail.
