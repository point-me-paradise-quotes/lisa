Your trip at a glance

Two ways to honeymoon in the Aegean

Both voyages leave in the same week of April, start in Athens, and sail between the Greek islands and the Turkish coast. In both you have a balcony suite and premium economy seats across the Atlantic. Where they differ is the ship, the route and how much is already paid for.

Option A, Celestyal Journey, April 7 to 18. Seven nights round trip from Athens to Ephesus, Rhodes, Crete, Santorini, Mykonos and Milos. A guided tour in every port, a honeymoon package, a drinks credit, two specialty dinners and spa treatments are already included, and you stay at the same Piraeus hotel before and after the cruise.

Option B, Windstar Star Legend, April 6 to 18. Eight nights on a small yacht-style ship from Athens through the Corinth Canal to Delphi, Santorini, Bodrum, Ephesus, Mykonos and Lemnos, ending in Istanbul. The suite is larger, and drinks, Wi-Fi and gratuities are included. Shore tours are extra, apart from a private Evening in Ephesus dinner. You spend two nights in Athens before and one at the Pera Palace in Istanbul after.

Choose either option below to see its full day-by-day plan, suite, flights and pricing.
